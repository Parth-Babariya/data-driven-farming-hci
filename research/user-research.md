# User Research

The research focused on the everyday decisions farmers make across crop planning, weather response, irrigation, pest control, financing, selling produce, and learning.

## Key needs

- Reliable local weather, rainfall, temperature, humidity, and soil information.
- Practical guidance for crop selection, irrigation, pests, diseases, and harvest timing.
- Current market prices and a way to sell produce with less wastage.
- Easier access to loans, subsidies, government schemes, storage, transport, and helplines.
- Learning content that combines demonstrations, interactive modules, FAQs, and traditional farming knowledge.
- Peer support through farmer forums, feedback, and community stories.
- Support for regional languages, low-connectivity/offline use, notifications, and privacy controls.

## Common pain points

- Inaccurate rainfall information can cause over-irrigation or wasted pesticide applications.
- Limited access to expert advice and complex government processes slow down decisions.
- High input costs and uncertain weather make investment risky.
- Pest pressure, water scarcity, unstable prices, and limited storage affect income.
- Farmers need information at the right time, in a format that is easy to understand and act on.

## Design implications

The proposed experience brings time-sensitive information into a dashboard and separates full signed-in features from public access. It prioritizes clear navigation, actionable alerts, simple language, visual learning, and multiple channels for raising issues or getting help.
