# User Personas

## Primary persona: Ramesh Tiwari

- **Occupation:** Progressive farmer
- **Location:** Nashik, Maharashtra, India
- **Crop:** Grapes
- **Land area:** 5 acres
- **Education:** Graduate
- **Technology:** Mobile phone, WhatsApp, Facebook, YouTube, weather apps, and agricultural apps

### Motivations

- Produce export-quality crops using resources efficiently.
- Improve output from existing land.
- Sell more of the harvest with less wastage.
- Experiment with profitable crops.

### Goals

- Protect crops from weather, pests, and diseases.
- Learn how to identify and manage pests and diseases.
- Choose harvest timing using market and weather information.
- Track crop growth, soil conditions, expenses, and resource use.
- Learn from experienced farmers.

### Frustrations

- Pesticide applications can be wasted by rain or wind.
- Incorrect rainfall information can cause over-irrigation and crop damage.
- Excess chemicals can reduce crop quality and exportability.
- Excess water and input use increase costs.

### Representative quote

> “I am very busy throughout the whole day. I use the app in the morning to plan out my work.”

## Broader user themes

The research materials also represent farmers with different levels of experience, technology access, and confidence. The product should therefore support both quick, information-dense workflows for experienced users and guided, visual workflows for users who need more explanation.
